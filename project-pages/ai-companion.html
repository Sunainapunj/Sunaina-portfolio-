AI Companion for Older Adults

UX Research · Accessible Technology · AI Interaction

Conceptual illustration, not a real product screen.

The challenge

As mobile interfaces evolve, some older adults may find it difficult to adapt to changing navigation patterns, small touch targets, complex menus, unfamiliar icons, dense information, and multi-step digital tasks.

These challenges can make routine activities frustrating and can increase reliance on family members or other people for assistance. This project treats them as questions to investigate, not claims about every older adult.

The design opportunity

An accessible, supportive AI companion that helps people understand and complete digital tasks with greater confidence and independence, while keeping them in control of important decisions.

Potential interaction directions

Voice guidance

Ask and be guided by speaking, in a preferred language.

Simple instructions

Clear, plain-language explanations of what is on screen.

Readable UI

Larger text, strong contrast, and generous touch targets.

Step-by-step assistance

Multi-step tasks broken into one confirmed action at a time.

Multilingual support

Help that works in the language people are most comfortable with.

Research direction

Understand older adults' needs through research, then validate design decisions through usability testing. Open questions include which tasks cause the most friction, when guidance helps versus intrudes, and how to keep people in control.

An accessible digital guide is in development.
